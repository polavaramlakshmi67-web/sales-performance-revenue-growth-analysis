# Sales Performance & Revenue Growth Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance, profitability, product performance, and regional trends to help business stakeholders understand revenue patterns and identify opportunities for improvement.

Using data analysis and interactive Power BI visualizations, the project provides a business-focused view of key performance indicators (KPIs), sales trends, and profit contribution across different business dimensions.

## Business Problem

Businesses need to understand whether sales growth is translating into profitability, which products and categories contribute most to performance, and which regions or customer segments require attention.

Without a clear view of these metrics, decision-makers may struggle to identify underperforming areas and prioritize improvement opportunities.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, order volume, and quantity sold.
- Track monthly sales trends and identify changes over time.
- Compare sales and profitability across regions.
- Identify top-performing products by sales.
- Evaluate sales contribution across product categories and customer segments.
- Build an interactive dashboard to support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** — Dashboard development, data visualization, and KPI reporting
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation
- **DAX** — Measures and business calculations
- **Microsoft Excel** — Data inspection and preparation
- **SQL** — Data exploration and analytical queries

## Dataset

The project uses the Sample Superstore dataset, which contains sales transaction information such as order dates, product categories, customer segments, geographical regions, sales, profit, and quantity.

The dataset supports analysis of sales trends, profitability, product performance, and regional differences.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Understanding:** Reviewed the dataset structure and relevant business fields.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Measures overall sales value |
| Total Profit | Measures overall profit generated |
| Total Orders | Tracks order volume |
| Total Quantity | Measures units sold |
| Profit Margin | Measures profit as a percentage of sales |

**Profit Margin Formula**

Profit Margin = Total Profit / Total Sales × 100

## Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Quantity, and Profit Margin
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Examines how sales change over time
- **Top 5 Products by Sales:** Highlights products with the highest sales values
- **Profit by Region:** Compares profitability across geographical regions
- **Sales by Customer Segment:** Compares sales contribution across customer segments
- **Sales by Category:** Shows sales distribution across product categories
- **Interactive Slicers:** Region, Order Date, Category, and Segment

These features allow users to explore the data from different business perspectives.
